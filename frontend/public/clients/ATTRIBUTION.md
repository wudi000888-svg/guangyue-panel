## Shadowrocket identifier

`shadowrocket-identifier.svg` is an original “SR” typographic identifier created for Guangyue Panel under this repository's LGPL-3.0-only license. It is **not** the official Shadowrocket app icon. No Shadowrocket artwork is redistributed because a reusable license was not established. The client links directly to its official App Store listing.
